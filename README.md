This repository contains:

1. An appendix presenting the complete lists of municipalities surveyed within the ASDV, of those for which no recordings are available, and of the fusion of municipalities that have affected the provinces of Veneto after the 1980s. The lists are supplemented with additional information for each municipality, such as the year of recording and the age of the informants, drawn from the accompanying documentation.

2. A draft version for the visualisation of the municipalities of Veneto through interactive maps. The website is available at: [https://marcolonghin.github.io/ASDV-OArJ](https://marcolonghin.github.io/ASDV-OArJ).
